# Changelog

## 0.15.3 — first public beta

- Keyboard/mouse backpack and side-by-side personal-bank grids with search, scrolling and native item actions.
- Searchable character, equipment and account-bank snapshots; current-character inventory stays live.
- Embedded Advanced Filters plus optional inventory decorators and knowledge integrations.
- Explicit item delivery planning, configurable repeat rules, Any-source rules, protected items, profession profiles, recipe/research suggestions, deconstruction delivery and bank overflow routing.
- Gold display, native bank currency actions and planned gold policies.
- Known-recipe ingredient previews, selected-source confirmation and ingredient holds.
- Native profession material boundaries, separate style/trait categories and furniture-material retention.
- Failure-aware history, delivery overview, rule explanations, mouseover help, pause, read-only preview and configuration backup/import.
- Reconcile exact unique items already on the intended recipient, including duplicate old source/bank plans, without issuing another move.

The beta packages the tested 0.15.3 runtime. It remains a beta: broader player setups and addon combinations need testing. No player settings, inventories or third-party libraries are included.
